# SKODA-703 accessibility audit (WCAG 2.1 AA), 2026-10-15

Ticket: [SKODA-703](../tickets/tickets/SKODA-703.md) (#43). Branch `skoda-703-a11y` (from `main` at `cf98249`).
Method: measured, no screenshots. axe-core 4.14.0 (WCAG 2.0 / 2.1 A + AA rules) in Chromium via Playwright at 375 and
1280:
- **Baseline:** every M1 page (134) on the `main` preview and on the live source (same paths).
- **Verification:** the **coverage set**, 18 pages that together hold every template (7), block (15), block variant (16)
  and section style (11) in the M1 content (greedy set cover, `.migration/skoda-703/coverage-set.mjs`), plus the
  runtime-built parts the fixes touch (YouTube facade, story slider, video listing, Media Room home and footer, archive
  band, a Classic Days chapter); `main` vs the branch (local AEM against the same DA content).
- **Keyboard / focus / ARIA:** a scripted pass on the 9 template URLs (the PR #288 set) at 1280 and 375.
Screen-reader behaviour beyond the accessibility tree needs the human pass in §6.

`aria-input-field-name`, `frame-title`). One finding was EDS-introduced: `label-content-name-mismatch` on the locale links
(fixed, §2).

## 5. Empty alts (SKODA-501)
134 pages, 3,236 images, **149 empty alts (4.6%)** on 36 pages:
- **140 are correct as decorative:** card / tile images whose card has a visible title (131), series-hub hero images under
  the h1 (5), story portraits next to their name caption (3), one New Fabia image beside its text (1).
- **9 lone linked images** (the link's only content) made their link nameless: backfilled by the importer (§2).
  - **Author fix:** "Škoda Auto celebrates more than 130 years of craftsmanship and innovation" (Classic Days 2026) was
    edited in DA after the last import, so it wasn't overwritten; its body photo link still needs an alt in DA.
